# Finance Fundamentals

## Income Statement

The income statement shows the company's revenue,
expenses and profit over a period of time.

Basic relationship:

Revenue - Expenses = Profit

---

## Balance Sheet

The balance sheet shows what a company owns and owes
at a particular point in time.

The basic accounting equation is:

Assets = Liabilities + Equity

### Assets
Things the company owns or controls.

Examples:
- Cash
- Inventory
- Property
- Equipment

### Liabilities
Amounts the company owes.

Examples:
- Loans
- Accounts payable

### Equity
The owner's/shareholders' interest in the company.

---

## Cash Flow Statement

The cash flow statement shows how cash moves in and out
of the business.

It has three main sections:

1. Operating Activities
2. Investing Activities
3. Financing Activities
